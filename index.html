01 — ABOUT

Research at the intersection of AI and the built environment.

I am a researcher at Pusan National University, with a background in architectural engineering and construction management. I study AI methods that work with the complex visual and documentary evidence found in construction projects.

My work spans quality-aware synthetic image augmentation for construction safety object detection, as well as emerging research on evidence-grounded language models for construction supervision and project-state reasoning.
