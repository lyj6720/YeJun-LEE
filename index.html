01 — ABOUT

Research at the intersection of vision, quality, and reasoning.

My research centers on construction AI. I investigate how machine learning systems can better interpret construction scenes and project documents, with a particular emphasis on reliability, evidence quality, and practical usefulness.

My recent work examines how the quality of synthetic images affects downstream object detection. I also study retrieval-augmented reasoning over construction supervision documents to support traceable, project-state-aware question answering.

Synthetic Data Filtering for Enhanced Construction Object Detection via Image Quality Assessment

Y. Lee, J. Lee, F. Baek, H. Kim, J. Kim, and J. Jeon

This manuscript investigates how generative image quality influences construction object detection. It proposes an IQA-based synthetic-data curation framework and evaluates the effects of high-quality versus low-quality generated images for safety helmet and excavator detection.

03 — RESEARCH

Current research directions.

My work spans construction safety computer vision, synthetic-data curation, and evidence-grounded language models.

RESEARCH 012025–2026

Quality-aware synthetic data for construction object detection

Studying how image quality assessment can guide the selection of AI-generated images for object detection in safety-critical construction contexts.

IQA · SYNTHETIC DATA CURATION · OBJECT DETECTION
RESEARCH 022024–2025

Generative augmentation for construction hazard detection

Exploring text-to-image augmentation to address data scarcity in construction safety monitoring and to enhance the robustness of hazard-related recognition models.

GENERATIVE AI · CONSTRUCTION SAFETY · COMPUTER VISION
RESEARCH 03ONGOING

LLM + RAG for construction supervision

Developing an evidence-grounded framework that retrieves relevant documents, reasons over time and procedural roles, and generates cited responses for construction supervision tasks.

  • Focus on personnel assignments, material approvals, and project-state reasoning.
  • Goal: traceable Q&A grounded in real project documents.
  • Methodological emphasis: retrieval, temporal context, and evidence conflict resolution.
