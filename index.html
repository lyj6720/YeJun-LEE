About Me

Hi, I’m Yejun Lee.

I am a construction AI researcher at Pusan National University, working with Prof. JungHo Jeon in architectural engineering and construction management.

My research explores artificial intelligence for construction management, with a focus on computer vision and generative data augmentation for construction safety. I study how synthetic-image quality affects object detection, and how LLMs can reason over construction supervision documents to support evidence-based decisions.
